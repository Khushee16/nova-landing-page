Features

One workspace for every number your team cares about

Dashboards, reports, alerts, and integrations — designed so the whole team works from the same source of truth.

Real-time dashboards

Drag-and-drop dashboards that update live as your data changes. Share a link and everyone sees the same numbers.

  • Live refresh, no manual rebuilds
  • 50+ chart types and KPI tiles
  • Shareable links with view-only access

Scheduled reports

Send polished weekly and monthly reports to stakeholders automatically — no more screenshot marathons.

  • Daily, weekly, or monthly schedules
  • PDF and email delivery
  • Branded templates included

Team workspaces

Organize metrics by team with roles and permissions, so marketing, product, and finance each get their view.

  • Role-based access per workspace
  • Shared metric definitions
  • Comments and annotations on charts

Smart alerts

Get pinged in Slack or email when a metric moves unexpectedly — catch problems before your customers do.

  • Anomaly detection on any metric
  • Slack, email, and webhook delivery
  • Thresholds and quiet hours

40+ integrations

Connect your warehouse, CRM, billing, and product tools in minutes with native, maintained connectors.

  • Snowflake, BigQuery, Redshift, Postgres
  • Salesforce, HubSpot, Stripe, and more
  • REST API and CSV import

Enterprise-grade security

SOC 2 Type II certified with SSO, audit logs, and region-pinned data residency for regulated teams.

  • SSO / SAML and SCIM provisioning
  • Full audit logs
  • Encryption in transit and at rest
